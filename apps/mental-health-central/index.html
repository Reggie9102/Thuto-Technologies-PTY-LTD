Learning library

Understand What You're Going Through

Trusted, science-backed resources written for students.

😰

Anxiety & Panic

What it is, why it happens, and 7 proven ways to calm your mind.

  • Name it: "this is anxiety, not danger."
  • Box breathing (4-4-4-4) in your console.
  • 5-4-3-2-1 grounding: see, touch, hear, smell, taste.
  • Cut caffeine after 14:00.
😔

Depression

You're not weak, you're not alone. Here's what helps.

  • Small wins count: shower, walk, one message.
  • Sunlight before 10:00 lifts mood chemistry.
  • Free counselling is one tap away — use it.
😴

Sleep Better

The student sleep guide. Fall asleep faster, wake up refreshed.

  • Same wake-up time, even weekends.
  • Screens down 30 min before bed.
  • Bed = sleep only, not studying.
📚

Exam Stress

Healthy study habits that reduce anxiety and boost performance.

  • 25-min focus blocks, 5-min movement breaks.
  • Practice papers > re-reading notes.
  • Pair your study plan with your energy: open your Fundisa AI workspace from the console bridge.
💗

Relationships

Healthy vs unhealthy. Boundaries. Breakups. Friendship drama.

  • Boundaries are respect, not rejection.
  • Conflict: attack the problem, not the person.
  • Any fear in a relationship = get support now.
🏠

Family Issues

When home is hard. Coping with conflict, loss, and responsibility.

  • You can love family and still need space.
  • Peer supporters get it — talk to one.
  • Home violence? GBV Command Centre: 0800 428 428.
💸

Money Stress

Financial anxiety is real. Resources, bursaries, and support.

  • Campus financial aid offices exist for exactly this.
  • StudentMart SA lists subsidised devices & data.
  • Write the number down — fear shrinks when it's concrete.
🌈

Identity & LGBTQ+

Safe spaces, affirming resources, and community support.

  • You belong, exactly as you are.
  • Affirming counsellors available via free counselling.
  • Peer support groups on most campuses.
