FAQ

Questions Before You Start

Is MatricMate AI aligned with both NSC and IEB curricula?

Yes. Every past paper question, quiz, and study guide in MatricMate AI is tagged for NSC, IEB, or both. When you sign up, you select your exam body and all content filters accordingly. We work directly with the DBE and IEB to ensure our content matches the latest ATPs and mark schemes.

How does the multilingual AI work?

Our multilingual AI is powered by InkubaLM and NLLB-200, two of the world's most advanced African language models. You can switch between English and any of the 10 other official SA languages mid-question. The AI translates the question, your answer, and the explanation — so you can understand concepts in your home language while practicing exam-style English.

How do I book a one-on-one tutor?

Go to the Tutors tab in the app, filter by subject and availability, and click "Book". You can pay per session via Yoco, Zapper, or EFT. MatricMate Pro subscribers get 2 free tutor sessions per month and 30% off any additional sessions. Tutors are sourced from StudentMart SA (graduate tutors) and Frictionless Learner (peer tutors).

What's the difference between StudentMart tutors and Frictionless Learner peer tutors?

StudentMart tutors are university students with proven matric distinctions (R80–R250/hour). Frictionless Learner peer tutors are current matrics who aced the same subjects you're studying (R60–R80/hour). Peer tutors are great for study-buddy-style learning; graduate tutors are ideal for deeper conceptual work and exam technique.

How do schools book the Matric Bootcamp?

SGB chairs, principals, or HODs can book the Matric Bootcamp (R2,500/learner for 10 days) or Pre-Tertiary Readiness Camp (R3,500/learner for 5 days) via our schools portal. We issue official tax invoices and can work with your school's finance officer. Bulk discounts apply for 2+ cohorts. The bootcamp can be delivered online or face-to-face at your school.

Can I get a bursary if I'm on a SASSA grant?

Yes. SASSA Child Support Grant recipients get 85% off MatricMate Pro (just R30/month). Rural/Quintile 1-3 school learners get 70% off. NSFAS-eligible learners get 100% free access. Verification takes 60 seconds via our POPIA-compliant form — we hash your SASSA reference number and never store it.

Does the app work offline during load-shedding?

Yes. MatricMate AI is built offline-first. You can download up to 500 past paper questions, 50 video lessons, and all your quizzes for offline use. When power or data returns, your progress syncs automatically. The app uses only ~50MB of data per month — ideal for SA data costs.

Is my data protected under POPIA?

Yes. We are a PAIA-registered Responsible Party (REGGIE EXPRESS Pty Ltd, K2024698680). All learner data is processed under explicit consent, stored in encrypted form, and never sold or shared. You can request access, correction, or deletion at any time. Our Information Officer is available for any data subject queries.

What career guidance is available?

Mental Health Central provides HPCSA-registered career psychologists who offer psychometric assessments, degree selection guidance, university comparisons, and NSFAS/bursary application support. Sessions cost R350 and are included free for MatricMate Unlimited subscribers (4 sessions per term). Mental health support is always 100% free.
