Frequently Asked

Your Questions, Answered

Who is this academy for?

Thuto AI Academy is designed for two main audiences: (1) in-service teachers in SA primary and high schools who want to integrate AI into their teaching while earning SACE-recognised CPD credits, and (2) postgraduate students (MEd, PhD) researching AI in education. School leaders (principals, HODs, SGB chairs) are also welcome on our Leadership pathway.

Are the certifications really ETDP SETA-aligned?

Yes. Our programmes are developed in consultation with ETDP SETA and MICT SETA and align with the unit standards for AI-enhanced teaching. CPD credits are recognised by SACE. Full accreditation certification is in progress — we are currently operating under provisional alignment with full ETDP SETA accreditation targeted for Q4 2025.

Do I need technical skills or coding experience?

No. Our Foundation pathway assumes zero technical background. You'll learn to use no-code AI tools like Fundisa AI, MatricMate AI, and ChatGPT through prompt engineering. The Intermediate and Advanced pathways build on this foundation with more sophisticated techniques.

Can my school pay for my training?

Yes — most SA schools can claim teacher training from their Skills Development Levy (1% of payroll). We provide all the documentation your HR or SGB needs, including tax invoices, SACE CPD certificates, and ETDP SETA alignment letters. For institutional licenses (10+ teachers), we offer volume discounts and on-site delivery.

What's the difference between online, face-to-face, and blended?

Online: Self-paced LMS access with weekly live Q&As — ideal if you're balancing full-time teaching. Face-to-face: In-person at one of our 5 campuses, small cohorts (max 20), hands-on workshops. Blended: Online modules during the week + monthly Saturday workshops. All three deliver the same content and CPD credits; the blended model is our most popular option.

What happens if I can't attend a face-to-face session?

All face-to-face sessions are recorded and uploaded to your LMS within 24 hours. You can also transfer to a later cohort at no additional cost, provided you notify us 7 days in advance. We understand teachers' schedules are unpredictable.

Do you offer subsidies for rural or township schools?

Yes. Teachers at Quintile 1-3 schools receive 50% off all pathways, funded through the Thuto Student Funding Pool (sourced from 2.5% of StudentMart transactions). NSFAS-eligible postgrads may also qualify for bursary support. Contact us with your school's EMIS code to verify eligibility.

What AI tools will I learn to use?

You'll work hands-on with: Fundisa AI (lesson generation for R-7), MatricMate AI (NSC/IEB prep), Qwen 3.6 (English AI), InkubaLM (African languages), NLLB-200 (translation), SuperviseAI (postgrad research), and mainstream tools like ChatGPT, Claude, and Google Gemini. All taught in the SA educational context.
