Frequently Asked

Questions Before You Enroll

Do I need a technical background to enroll?

No. Our Foundation and Intermediate courses are designed for educators with no technical background. You just need a laptop or smartphone and basic digital literacy. Specialist courses (NQF 7–8) assume some research or technical familiarity, and we offer a free bridging module if needed.

Will the SACE CPD points actually count for my licence renewal?

Yes. All our teacher-facing courses are submitted to SACE for CPD Category A recognition. You receive a formal CPD certificate with a unique SACE reference number, which you submit through the SACE portal during your 5-year renewal cycle. Points are valid from date of issue.

Can my school or employer pay for my course?

Absolutely. We issue official tax invoices and can work directly with your school's finance officer or employer's HR department. For ETDP SETA-levied employers, we provide the WSP/ATR documentation needed to claim back up to 20% of your skills levy. Bulk discounts apply for groups of 5+.

Are the face-to-face workshops available in my province?

We run face-to-face intensives in all 9 provinces: Johannesburg, Cape Town, Durban, Pretoria, Port Elizabeth, Bloemfontein, Polokwane, Nelspruit, and Kimberley. Cohorts run monthly in major metros and quarterly in smaller hubs. Online learners anywhere in SA (or abroad) can join any cohort.

As a postgraduate, can I use these credits towards my degree?

Yes, subject to your university's RPL (Recognition of Prior Learning) policies. Our NQF 7 and 8 courses are designed to be stackable — we have existing credit transfer agreements with partner universities for MEd, PGCE, and MSc programmes. We provide full course documentation (learning outcomes, assessment criteria, moderation reports) to support your RPL application.

What happens if I don't pass the Portfolio of Evidence?

Every learner gets one free resubmission with written feedback from assessors. We maintain a 94% first-attempt pass rate, and our mentors offer weekly office hours to help you prepare. If you need more support, we offer a paid mentorship extension (R950) with dedicated 1-on-1 guidance.

Is my data protected under POPIA?

Yes. We are a PAIA-registered Responsible Party (REGGIE EXPRESS Pty Ltd, K2024698680). All learner data is processed under explicit consent, stored in encrypted form, and never sold or shared. You can request access, correction, or deletion of your data at any time through our POPIA portal. Our Information Officer is available for any data subject queries.

Do you offer bursaries for rural teachers?

Yes. We reserve 20% of places in every cohort for bursary-funded learners — prioritising Quintile 1–3 schools, rural provinces, and NSFAS alumni. Bursaries cover 50–100% of course fees depending on need. Apply through the standard enrollment form and select "Apply for Bursary" — we'll assess based on your school's EMIS code and household income.
